MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between: Northwind Technologies Pvt Ltd ("Client")
And:     WorkLedger Staffing Services ("Vendor")

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TERM

This Agreement is effective from 1 April 2026 and shall expire on
31 March 2027 unless renewed in writing by both parties.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RATES

The hourly rate for Senior Engineers shall be INR 2,400 per hour.
Junior Engineers are billed at INR 1,100 per hour.
Overtime, where pre-approved in writing, is billed at 1.5 x the standard rate.
The total contract value shall not exceed INR 9,600,000.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BILLING AND PAYMENT

Engagement is on a time and materials basis.
Invoices shall be raised monthly in arrears, itemised by role and by resource.
Invoices are payable within 45 days of receipt.
Late payment attracts interest of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

Only hours recorded in the Vendor's timesheet system and approved by the
Client's delegated manager are eligible for invoicing. Unapproved hours shall
not be billed under any circumstances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MILESTONES

Milestone 1 - Discovery and solution design signed off: INR 450,000
Milestone 2 - Integration environment delivered: INR 675,000
Milestone 3 - Production release accepted by Client: INR 900,000

Milestone payments become due only on written acceptance by the Client.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days prior written notice.
Work completed and approved before the effective date of termination remains
payable under the terms of Section 3.
